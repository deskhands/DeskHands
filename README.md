# DeskHands

## Use Web AI for office work and coding on your Mac.

### No separate API tokens to buy. No per-token charges from DeskHands.

**用网页 AI 在 Mac 上干活，办公、编程都可以。连接 ChatGPT 无需另购 API Token，DeskHands 不按 Token 收费。**

An all-in-one AI app that connects **ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and AI agents** to your files, browser and local tools. Create documents, analyze spreadsheets, write code and get the finished files back in your conversation.

**Connect ChatGPT and work directly from your browser — no separate API token purchase needed.** Your AI provider's plan and usage limits still apply; other AI connections follow their own requirements.

**[Download DeskHands for macOS](https://deskhands.app/?lang=en) · [Setup guide](https://deskhands.app/docs.html) · [Pricing](https://deskhands.app/?lang=en#pricing)**

## One app. Real work.

- **Office documents:** create and edit Word documents, Excel workbooks and PowerPoint presentations.
- **Data analysis:** work with local CSV and Excel files, clean data, build formulas and turn results into reports.
- **PDF & typesetting:** produce reports with charts, equations and professional page layouts.
- **Browser automation:** find sources, read web pages, click and fill forms.
- **Files & coding:** organize local files, read projects, edit code, run commands and check the results.
- **Work from your phone:** send tasks through your connected AI, let your Mac do the work and download the results in chat. Your Mac needs to stay awake, online and running DeskHands.

## Tell your AI what you need

> Analyze this sales spreadsheet, explain what changed and create a report with charts.

> Turn these notes into a Word report and a PowerPoint presentation.

> Read this project, fix the bug and run the tests.

DeskHands brings the tools to your Mac. You give instructions through the AI you already use. Available features depend on your connected AI, permissions and task setup.

## For AI FDEs and consultants

Show a team how AI can handle the files, data and reports they work with every day. Leave them with a way to repeat the task through conversation on their own Mac.
